I am a **first-year PhD student at City University of Hong Kong**, and I am very fortunate to be advised by [**Prof. Kaidi Xu**](https://kaidixu.com/). I completed my master's study at Wuhan University, where I was advised by [Prof. Chong Teng](https://jszy.whu.edu.cn/tengchong/zh_CN/index.htm).

My research interests focus on <u>uncertainty estimation for language models</u> and **improving reliability in multi-step decision-making tasks**. In particular, I study how language models can recognize and manage uncertainty across **MoE routing**, **tool use**, and **agentic planning**, where models need to make a sequence of decisions rather than a single prediction.

## Academic Awards

Our work [**DiscoSG**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15583) received the [**EMNLP 2025 Outstanding Paper Award**](http://cips-cl.org/?p=88817). I am especially grateful to [Prof. Zhuang Li](https://zhuang-li.github.io/) for the collaboration and his valuable support on this work.

## Collaboration

I warmly welcome **research collaborations** in related areas. I am also happy to work with <u>junior students who are interested in starting research on LLMs</u>. Please feel free to reach out at [kyrielincn@gmail.com](mailto:kyrielincn@gmail.com).
